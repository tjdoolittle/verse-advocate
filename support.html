Verse & Advocate finds Bible verses that speak to whatever you describe, in plain English, entirely offline. If something is not working or a verse you expected did not appear, this page should help.

This is a free app built by one person, so replies are not instant, but every message is read.

Using the app

Describe the idea, not the words

Search the way you would say it out loud — "forgiveness is better than revenge" works better than a single keyword. The app maps everyday language to biblical vocabulary, so "revenge" also finds "vengeance", and "worry" also finds "take no thought".

Always read the context

Every result has a Show context button that opens the surrounding verses. A verse can look like it says one thing on its own and something quite different in its passage. Use it before you rely on a line.

Copying a verse

Copy verse puts the text, the reference and the translation on your clipboard, ready to paste anywhere.

Common questions

My search found nothing

Try plainer, more common words. The app matches the vocabulary of the translation you have selected, so unusual or very modern phrasing can come up empty. The topic chips on the home screen are a good starting point, and shuffle will offer new ones.

Why does Tyndale find fewer verses?

William Tyndale did not translate the whole Bible. His 1534 text covers Genesis through Deuteronomy, Jonah, and the New Testament — nothing else exists to search. For whole-Bible coverage use the World English Bible or the King James Version.

Which translations are included, and why those?

The World English Bible (modern English), the King James Version (1611), and Tyndale (1534). All three are public domain, which is what makes it possible to bundle them in the app and search them offline at no cost. Most modern translations are licensed and cannot be included.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes, completely. Every verse is stored inside the app. Nothing is downloaded and nothing is sent anywhere, so it works the same in airplane mode.

What does the relevance number mean?

It is how strongly a verse matched what you typed. Results favour shorter, more memorable verses, and each one lists the words it matched on so you can see why it surfaced.

Can it tell me which side of an argument a verse supports?

No, and it does not try to. It finds verses about your topic. Whether a verse actually says what you hoped is a question only reading the passage can answer — which is what Show context is for.

Privacy

The app collects nothing at all: no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no network requests. The full policy is on the privacy page.
